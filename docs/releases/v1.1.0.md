## ⪩ Mic Control v1.1.0 ⪨

Mic Control now updates itself from GitHub Releases and keeps its settings in a folder next to the exe.

> Copies of 1.0.0 cannot update themselves (they have no updater). Download `MicControl.exe` of this release once,
> quit the running 1.0.0 from its tray menu and replace the old exe. Your shortcut and appearance are imported on
> the first start.

---

### ⤷ Added ˎˊ˗

- **Self-update** — on every start, every 6 hours while it runs and on request, the app checks this repository's
  releases. A new version is downloaded in the background and verified by size and SHA-256 against `SHA256SUMS`.
  A notification "Mic Control X.Y.Z is ready" and the tray item **Restart to update** install it on a click: the
  app closes, the new exe replaces `MicControl.exe` in the same folder and starts in the tray, and a notification
  confirms "Updated to vX.Y.Z".
- **Download updates automatically** switch in Settings (on by default). Off: updates are only announced and
  downloaded on a click.
- Update status line and button in Settings (**Check for updates**, **Download**, **Restart to update**).
- Tray menu entries: the version, **Check for updates** and **Restart to update**.
- Every release ships `MicControl.exe` and `SHA256SUMS`.

### ⤷ Changed ˎˊ˗

- **Portable settings** — settings are stored in `cute-mic-control_data\settings.xml` next to `MicControl.exe`
  instead of `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Mic Control`. Settings of 1.0.0 are copied over on the first start; moving the exe
  together with that folder moves the whole installation.
- The self-test writes its report and previews to `cute-mic-control_data\self-test\`.
- The footer and the file properties name the author `softoblivion`.
