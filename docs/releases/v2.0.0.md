## ⪩ Mic Control v2.0.0 ⪨

Mic Control gets the shared look of the softoblivion desktop apps: four window themes, a darker background and one button system.

---

### ⤷ Added ˎˊ˗

- **Theme** row in Settings, above **Color palette**: the background colour and accent of the window. Four themes, each a hue for the window and one contrast accent for the primary action, the slider, the switches and the shortcut: **Dusk** (cobalt, coral accent), **Nova** (violet, ember accent), **Frost** (ice blue, lilac accent) and **Bloom** (magenta, mint accent; the default). A choice applies at once, with a short colour fade, and is saved; the arrow keys move the choice.

### ⤷ Changed ˎˊ˗

- **Darker window** — the settings window and the hotkey editor sit on a dark vertical gradient in the theme's hue, with a soft field of the hue drifting across the top and a smaller field of the accent in the bottom-right corner; the frame, its glow and the stars follow the hue. The tray menu takes the same background.
- **Buttons** — every button is a pill on a translucent plate (hairline, bevel, shadow and a light streak) with hover, pressed (a short squish) and keyboard-focus (a 2 px ring in the accent) states; disabled buttons dim. The window buttons show their plate on hover only.
- **Mute / Unmute** is the one tinted button: a gradient of the accent with a coloured glow.
- The heart indicator, its five colour palettes and the hotkey editor work as before.
