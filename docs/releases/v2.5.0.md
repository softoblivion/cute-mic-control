## ⪩ Mic Control v2.5.0 ⪨

The window becomes a small audio console: the microphone and the speakers side by side, each with mute, level, a live meter, the device choice and a level hold. Settings, the hotkey and the heart indicator carry over unchanged.

---

### ⤷ Added ˎˊ˗

- **Speakers** — a second card for the default output device: mute, level and device, like the microphone.
- **Levels and meters** — a level slider from 0 to 100 for each device, with a live peak meter under it; drag, click, scroll or use the arrow keys.
- **Device lists** — click a device name to make another microphone or output device the Windows default for every use, calls included (the communications default follows); **Windows sound settings** at the bottom opens the Windows page. The tray menu has the same lists under **Microphone** and **Speakers**.
- **Level hold** — the lock beside a level holds it: a change by Windows, a driver or another app is undone at once, and the lock shakes to show it. Holds are kept per device across restarts. A level Windows refuses to put back is reported once.
- **Listen** — plays the microphone through the speakers (the Windows "Listen to this device" option), to hear how you sound.

### ⤷ Changed ˎˊ˗

- **Main window** — two cards instead of the large heart and the wide Mute button; the microphone is muted with its round button, which glows in the palette's on colour while the microphone is live. The window is 420 x 456 at 100 % scale.
- **Desktop indicator** — the switch for the heart on the desktop moved into Settings → Appearance, above the indicator size, which is dimmed while the indicator is off.
- **Follows Windows** — a mute, level or device change made elsewhere shows up at once instead of within half a second.
- **Microphone-in-use icon** — while the window is open, Windows shows its microphone-in-use icon, because the level meter reads the microphone; closing the window to the tray releases it. Nothing is recorded.

### ⤷ Fixed ˎˊ˗

- **Updates** — an update check that fails on the network while a downloaded update waits keeps the update ready to install instead of showing an error.
